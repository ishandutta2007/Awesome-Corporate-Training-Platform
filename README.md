# Awesome-Corporate-Training-Platform

## Top Corporate Training Platform Tools Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Employee Learning Management Systems (LMS), L&D Platforms, Onboarding, Compliance Training, Skills Development & Extended Enterprise Learning*

**Last updated: August 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Corporate Training Platforms**. These tools help organizations deliver, track, and manage employee training, onboarding, compliance courses, skills development, collaborative learning, and extended-enterprise (partner/customer) training at scale.



**Examples** include 360Learning, Docebo, Absorb LMS, TalentLMS, Lessonly by Seismic, Trainual, LearnUpon, Bridge LMS, WorkRamp, and Northpass (the category leaders and widely adopted platforms).



**Open-source emphasis**: Corporate training has one of the strongest open-source ecosystems of any software category. The section below prioritizes mature, self-hostable LMS platforms that organizations can run without per-user licensing fees while retaining full control over data and customization.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites / GitHub repos.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[360Learning](https://360learning.com/)**  

  Collaborative corporate learning platform emphasizing peer-authored content, skills-based learning paths, and social learning for modern L&D teams.

- **[Docebo](https://www.docebo.com/)**  

  Enterprise AI-powered LMS with strong content management, personalization, extended enterprise capabilities, and advanced analytics.

- **[Absorb LMS](https://www.absorblms.com/)**  

  Modern, user-friendly corporate LMS known for clean UX, strong compliance and certification tracking, and AI-assisted features.

- **[TalentLMS](https://www.talentlms.com/)**  

  Popular mid-market and SMB-friendly LMS with fast setup, SCORM support, gamification, and affordable per-user pricing.

- **[Lessonly by Seismic](https://www.lessonly.com/)**  

  Enablement-focused training platform (now part of Seismic) strong in sales and customer-facing team training with practice and coaching features.

- **[Trainual](https://trainual.com/)**  

  Process and training documentation platform popular with growing companies for building playbooks, SOPs, and onboarding programs.

- **[LearnUpon](https://www.learnupon.com/)**  

  Highly rated LMS focused on ease of use, multi-portal architecture, and strong support for both employee and customer training.

- **[Bridge LMS](https://www.getbridge.com/)** (Instructure)  

  Corporate learning platform emphasizing performance, coaching, and integrated learning experiences.

- **[WorkRamp](https://www.workramp.com/)**  

  Enablement and training platform oriented toward revenue teams, customer education, and employee upskilling.

- **[Northpass](https://www.northpass.com/)**  

  Customer and employee training platform with strong content delivery and engagement features.

- **[SAP Litmos](https://www.litmos.com/)** / **[Cornerstone](https://www.cornerstoneondemand.com/)** / other enterprise suites  

  Additional large-scale platforms frequently evaluated for global compliance and talent management needs.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Moodle](https://github.com/moodle/moodle)**  

  The world’s most widely deployed open-source LMS (GPL). Extremely flexible with a massive plugin ecosystem (2,000+), strong SCORM/xAPI support, and proven use in corporate compliance, onboarding, and skills training. Moodle Workplace (commercial) adds multi-tenancy and corporate-oriented features on the same core.

- **[Open edX](https://github.com/openedx)**  

  Powerful open-source platform originally developed by Harvard and MIT. Excellent for large-scale course delivery, sophisticated learning pathways, and organizations with DevOps capacity that need high-volume training programs.

- **[Canvas LMS (Community Edition)](https://github.com/instructure/canvas-lms)**  

  Modern, intuitive open-source LMS (AGPL) with a clean interface. Widely used in education and adaptable for corporate environments that value usability and LTI integrations.

- **[Chamilo](https://github.com/chamilo/chamilo-lms)**  

  Lightweight, user-friendly open-source LMS focused on simplicity and fast deployment. Well suited for smaller corporate teams or organizations that want an easier alternative to Moodle.

- **[Forma LMS](https://www.formalms.org/)**  

  Open-source LMS specifically designed for corporate training needs (origins in the old Docebo open-source codebase). Supports internal training, extended enterprise, multi-client setups, and course catalogs with a business-oriented feature set.

- **[ILIAS](https://github.com/ILIAS-eLearning/ILIAS)**  

  Mature open-source LMS with strong role/permission controls and compliance-oriented capabilities, particularly popular in European and public-sector environments.

- **[Frappe Learning / ERPNext Education](https://github.com/frappe)**  

  Open-source learning and education modules built on the Frappe framework, useful for organizations already running ERPNext or seeking tightly integrated HR + training workflows.

- **Totara and related open-core corporate LMS distributions**  

  Corporate-focused distributions and extensions built on or inspired by Moodle that add talent and performance features.

- **SCORM/xAPI players and content tools**  

  Open-source components that enable standards-compliant course packages inside self-hosted LMS platforms.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Plugin ecosystems and themes that extend Moodle or Canvas for corporate branding, SSO, and advanced reporting.

- Open authoring tools (e.g., H5P, Adapt, eXeLearning) that produce content for any of the above LMS platforms.

- Skills frameworks and competency tracking modules that layer on top of open LMS cores.

- Integration connectors for HRIS, Slack/Teams, and SSO providers commonly used in corporate environments.

- Lightweight documentation + training hybrids built on open wikis or knowledge bases for process-heavy onboarding (complementary to full LMS).



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Most organizations start with **Moodle** (broadest ecosystem and plugin support) or **Forma LMS** (more corporate-native out of the box). **Chamilo** is preferred when simplicity and speed of deployment matter most. Pair any of these with open authoring tools and HRIS integrations to create a complete, self-hosted corporate training stack without per-seat licensing.

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Corporate training platforms should be evaluated for SCORM/xAPI support, reporting and compliance readiness, ease of content creation, mobile experience, multi-tenancy (for extended enterprise), SSO/HRIS integrations, and total cost of ownership (including self-hosting and administration effort).

- Open-source LMS platforms eliminate per-user fees and give full data ownership, but require technical resources for hosting, upgrades, security, and customization. Many organizations successfully run them with internal IT or certified partners.
